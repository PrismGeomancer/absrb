How it works

A second life,by vote.

Absorb is Merge's rescue cousin. It isn't two equals combining. A smaller, fading coin is folded into a stronger one, with protections for the host.

1
Nominate

Anyone proposes that a weak coin be absorbed by a stronger one. The terms are computed from live market data.

2
Vote

Holders of both coins vote for 24 hours, weighted by on-chain token balance. Both sides need a majority and a quorum.

3
Absorb

The target's liquidity joins the host's pool. Target holders swap into the host at the discounted ratio.

4
Close

The target coin is retired and its page reads "absorbed into $HOST". The community keeps its identity.

Swap discountTarget holders receive host tokens below the fair ratio, so existing host holders aren't diluted at full price.
VestingOnly a quarter of absorbed tokens unlock at execution. The rest unlock linearly, so they can't all hit the host's pool on day one.
Size gateA target can't exceed half of the host's market cap, so a host is never swallowed by something bigger.
Be honest about the risks. Someone could buy a dead coin cheap and push an absorption to dump on host holders. The discount, vesting, size gate and pre-proposal balance snapshots are the defenses. An absorption is a migration, not a guarantee the new tokens hold value.
